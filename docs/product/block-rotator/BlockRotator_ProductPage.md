## A Reverb That Breathes, Mutates, and Orbits

**Block Rotator** is an advanced algorithmic reverb and spatial effects processor that goes far beyond conventional reverb. It creates evolving, pitch-shifting reverb textures with complex spectral mutations and spatial rotations that transform over time. Whether you're crafting immersive ambient soundscapes, designing otherworldly sound effects, or adding deep, evolving dimension to your mix, Block Rotator delivers a class of reverb effects that simply cannot be achieved with traditional tools.

## Key Features

### 8-Channel Diffusion Delay Feedback Network

At the heart of Block Rotator is an 8-channel diffusion delay feedback network — a dense, multi-tap reverb architecture where eight parallel delay channels are continuously cross-fed and recirculated through spatial matrix transformations. This multi-channel topology produces far richer, more complex reverb textures than conventional stereo delay networks, with natural-sounding diffusion and a deep sense of three-dimensional space.

### 10 Proprietary Spatial Algorithms

Block Rotator features two distinct processing banks, each with five unique algorithms:

**Max Diffusion** — Spectral mutation algorithms that broaden and scatter your signal across the frequency spectrum, producing lush washes, dense clouds, and rich harmonic textures.

- Cyclops
- Apshai
- EyeDescent
- Scarab
- DeathIbis

**Max Rotation** — Spatial motion algorithms that create orbiting, spiraling, and morphing stereo fields with a sense of physical movement and rotation.

- BlockRotate
- Schistdisc
- BloodLoom
- Sekhmetal
- Uncoiling

Each algorithm represents a fundamentally different approach to how the internal signal channels interact and transform, giving you 10 completely distinct reverb characters.

### Pitch-Shifting Reverb Tails

The SSB (Single-Sideband) modulation engine applies real-time pitch shifting directly within the reverb's feedback network. Reverb tails don't just decay — they evolve, shimmer, and drift in pitch. Each successive reflection is further pitch-shifted, creating accumulating spectral drift that gives the reverb a living, breathing quality. Control the modulation frequency and depth to dial in anything from subtle detuning shimmer to dramatic pitch cascades.

### Resonant Multi-Mode Filter

Shape the reverb output with a resonant multi-mode filter featuring cutoff frequency, resonance (Q), mix level, and mode selection. Tame harsh frequencies, add warmth, create filtered sweeps, or use in combination with the morphing system for automated tonal movement.

### Parameter Morphing Automated Crossfader

Capture two complete parameter snapshots (A and B), then smoothly crossfade between them in real time. What makes Block Rotator's morphing system truly powerful:

- **Per-parameter control** — Enable or disable morphing independently for each parameter. Morph your room size while keeping reverb time fixed, or sweep the filter while the density stays locked.
- **LFO-driven crossfader** — Automate the morph with a built-in LFO. Choose from seven waveforms (Sine, Triangle, Saw+, Saw-, Square, Random, Sample & Hold) to shape the morphing motion.
- **Tempo sync** — Lock the crossfader LFO to your DAW's tempo with Free, Cycle, and Quantized sync modes for rhythmically locked parameter evolution.

### Early Reflections

An independent early reflections engine runs in parallel with the main reverb network, with dedicated inject level and delay time controls. Use it to add definition, presence, and spatial cues to complement the diffuse reverb tail.

### Real-Time Visualization

Four display modes give you instant visual feedback on your sound:

- **Spectrum** — Live FFT analysis with color-coded frequency bars
- **Waveform** — Real-time oscilloscope view of the audio signal
- **3D Spectrum** — Animated three-dimensional spectral surface
- **Phase Scope** — Stereo phase correlation meter for monitoring imaging

The visualizer features selectable color themes with 13 colormap options.

### Preset System

- Factory presets organized by category to get you started immediately
- Save, load, and manage your own user presets
- Full state recall including A/B captures and morphing configuration
- Quick-browse with previous/next navigation
